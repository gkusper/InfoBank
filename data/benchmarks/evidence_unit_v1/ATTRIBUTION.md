Joshua Poore, and Ziyu Yao. 2023. "MailEx: Email Event and Argument
Extraction." Proceedings of EMNLP 2023.

Project repository:

https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.801/

The derived benchmark is not endorsed by the MailEx authors. MailEx event
annotations were used for candidate discovery and provenance. InfoBank-specific
runtime schemas, matched D6-D8 design, D7 browser/search counterfactuals, and
OPEN/CLOSED/ABSENT gold labels were created for the InfoBank research prototype.
