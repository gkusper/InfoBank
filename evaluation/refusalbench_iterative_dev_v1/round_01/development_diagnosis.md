# Diagnosis after round 01

The initial implementation scores 55% CFAF accuracy (FULL recall 10%, CFAF recall 100%) on the new seeded sample. Seventeen queries remain UNRESOLVED. The shared control also scores 55% under the common error-inclusive cross-field scoring. This is a different random sample, not a causal comparison with the previous 50% pilot.

The dominant conceptual defect is insufficient generic factual coverage. v01 adds query-only WHO/WHERE/WHEN/WHAT/WHICH/HOW MUCH/HOW LONG answer typing and multipart requirements while preserving explicit InfoBank aggregate/metadata/action/individual-record scopes. A WHO question alone does not authorize or imply an individual record. No official record IDs or answers are used by these rules.

The previously documented generic-refusal and CFAF/FULL-status defects justify a simultaneous reason-template and validation correction. Fine internal causes use existing model properties; governance restrictions remain highest priority. Every generator gets the same cross-field expectations. Invalid raw outputs are retained as errors, and one system's invalid output no longer invalidates an otherwise valid peer response. The external primary cross-field scoring rule is identical across all rounds.

111 version-specific and existing governance tests passed before round 02; controlled InfoBank scenarios still distinguish aggregate FULL from individual restriction, and metadata FULL from restricted body text. No general internal contradiction detector or benchmark-answer exception was introduced.
